About The Club

Our Home

Prestige Park is where legends are made. Our state-of-the-art stadium holds 40,000 passionate fans who create an atmosphere unlike any other on matchday.

Our History

Founded with a vision of excellence, PUFC has grown from humble beginnings into one of the most respected clubs in the league. Our trophy cabinet tells the story of decades of dedication.

Our Squad

A blend of experienced veterans and exciting young talent, our squad embodies the spirit of Prestige Union — relentless, skillful, and united in pursuit of glory.

Why We Need Your Help

At PUFC, talent should never be held back by circumstance. Many of our dedicated players struggle to afford the essentials needed to represent the club in tournaments:

  • ⚽ Football jersey kits and match-day gear
  • 🚌 Transportation to away games and tournaments
  • 🍽️ Meals and nutrition during competition days

Every contribution — no matter how small — helps a player step onto the pitch with confidence and pride. Together, we can ensure no one is left behind.
