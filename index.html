About The Club

Our Philosophy

Respect the Field

Giving importance to the field will give each player not only sports etiquette but life skills as well. Treating the field as your home, in which all fundamentals of learning are honed and constant progress is achieved each time you step into it. The field is not just a rectangular pitch with lines, markers, and goal posts, we teach our players that giving respect in and outside of the playing field should be part of life. Taking care of the field means taking care of PUFC. When everyone from the club progresses in life, they will surely choose different fields, and the only way to become successful in any path of life is to nurture the skills, take good care of their passion, and have faith.

Respect the Team

We call our team not just a "union", but a "prestige union" — the convergence of different people joined together in a bond of passion for football, makes the group not just a team, but a UNION. We believe that everyone has different sets of skills and talents that are unique to them and contributes to the success of the team. We do not blame our teammates, we do not curse, and we do not belittle. Everyone is important and mostly their abilities blossoms in their own perfect timing as a result of their hard work, dedication, and their openness to learn.

Respect the Game

Though football is a competitive sport, and being aggressive and always on the attack mode is second nature in this type of sport, we always encourage each and everyone to play fairly, with finesse, and with high regard towards others. Skills are best developed in ways where eagerness to learn comes first and attitude is everything. Through this mindset, players deviate from wrong behavior, frustrations, disappointments, and most importantly, from inflicting injuries to the team, opponents, and one's own self. Treating the beautiful game with respect will surely give the players the respect they deserve.

Play Strong

Our Mantra

A guiding philosophy of Prestige Union Football Club that promotes playing with purpose, resilience, and character — developing not just better athletes, but stronger individuals on and off the field.

PUFC's 3E Framework

The 3E Framework is a holistic player development approach centered on Experience, Exposure, and Empowerment — designed to build skills, broaden opportunities, and shape confident, disciplined individuals both on and off the field.

Experience

Providing consistent, meaningful game and training opportunities where players learn by doing, building confidence and real understanding of the sport.

Exposure

Connecting players to different levels of competition, coaching styles, and football environments to broaden their perspective and elevate their game. We believe that exposing players to real games will fast track their learning and playing capabilities even at any level of playing skills.

Empowerment

Instilling discipline, leadership, and self-belief so players take ownership of their growth and become responsible individuals beyond football.

Social sporting and community building are what set us apart from other local amateur teams. We make sure that we share this beautiful game to all, especially to those who are eager to play but are bound by their financial limitations. We are here to offer anyone a union of team play, friendship, and a shared vision of extending an open arm to our beneficiaries.

Our Mission

Through our united views of playing and sharing beautiful games, we help build and develop a strong foundation for young champions to succeed, not only to score in any sports but in their personal life goals as well

Our Vision

"We believe that through social sporting, Prestige Union FC will be among the prime football clubs in the country to provide support and elevate the Filipino football culture."

"To be the home of passionate football enthusiasts doing it for the love of the game while bringing sports and wellness closer to the community."

Year Round Program

A structured, all-year football development journey designed to build skills, character, and game experience — balanced with a seasonal break from September 15 to October 15 to promote recovery, reflection, and the belief that growth also comes from rest, because too much of anything can be counterproductive.

>

Basic Camp

January – March

Focuses on football fundamentals — building strong technical skills and proper habits from the ground up.

Summer Camp

April – May

An intensive and fun learning environment that accelerates player development through consistent training and play.

Bootcamp

June – September

High-energy training phase designed to improve fitness, discipline, and game readiness.

Team Camp

October – December

Prepares players for competition through team play, tactics, and real-game experience.
